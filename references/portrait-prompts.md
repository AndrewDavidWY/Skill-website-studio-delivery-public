## 通用要求 Shared Instructions

### 中文

以我上传的照片为人物身份参考，优先保留面部轮廓、五官比例、年龄感、肤色、发际线和个人辨识特征。不要换脸式美化，不要明显年轻化。保留真实皮肤纹理、细纹与自然表情。服装与造型适合照片中的本人，呈现可信、从容的企业董事长个人官网肖像风格。

### English

Use the uploaded photograph as the identity reference. Preserve the subject’s facial structure, proportions, apparent age, skin tone, hairline, and distinctive features. Avoid face-changing beautification or noticeable de-aging. Retain realistic skin texture, fine lines, and natural expressions. Choose clothing and styling appropriate to the person in the reference photo. Create a credible, composed, professional portrait for a company chairperson’s personal website.

## 1 官网首页 沉稳且有留白 Website Hero

### 中文

生成横版 16:9 商业编辑肖像。人物位于画面右侧，腰部以上构图，身体轻微侧转，自然看向镜头，神情平静、自信。穿剪裁合身的深海军蓝商务服装。背景为暖米白色现代空间，少量深铜色细节点缀。左侧保留约四成干净留白，用于后期排版标题。柔和侧窗光，自然人像透视，真实摄影质感，不生成文字。

### English

Create a horizontal 16:9 editorial business portrait. Position the subject on the right, framed from the waist up, with the body turned slightly and the gaze naturally directed toward the camera. The expression is calm and confident. Dress the subject in well-tailored deep navy business attire. Use a warm ivory contemporary interior with subtle dark bronze accents. Leave approximately 40% of the image on the left clean and uncluttered for a headline added later. Soft side-window lighting, natural portrait perspective, and realistic photographic detail. Do not generate any text.

## 2 正式简介 清晰可信 Formal Profile

### 中文

生成竖版 4:5 正式商业肖像，胸部以上构图，人物居中，头顶和双肩保留适当空间。深色简洁商务服装搭配暖白色内搭，无夸张配饰。表情从容，嘴角轻微放松，直视镜头。背景为低饱和暖灰米白色，柔和主光照亮双眼，阴影自然。呈现高质量企业年报人物摄影质感，保留皮肤细节。

### English

Create a vertical 4:5 professional business portrait, framed from the chest up. Center the subject with comfortable space above the head and around the shoulders. Use understated dark business attire with a warm white inner layer and minimal accessories. The subject looks directly into the camera with a composed expression and a subtly relaxed mouth. Use a muted warm gray or ivory background. Soft key lighting should illuminate the eyes while preserving natural shadows. Aim for the photographic quality of a professionally produced corporate annual report, with authentic skin detail.

## 3 关于我 亲和有温度 About Page

### 中文

生成竖版 4:5 自然商务肖像。人物穿无明显品牌标识的休闲西装与简洁内搭，坐在靠窗的浅色单椅上，上半身轻微前倾，像正在认真倾听，露出克制、真实的微笑。背景为安静明亮的现代办公休息区，家具简洁，轻度虚化。温暖自然日光，以肩部和上半身为主，体现亲和力与专业感。

### English

Create a vertical 4:5 natural business portrait. Dress the subject in a simple, unbranded blazer with an understated inner layer. Seat the subject in a light-colored chair near a window, leaning slightly forward as if listening attentively, with a subtle, genuine smile. The setting is a quiet, bright contemporary office lounge with simple furniture and a softly blurred background. Use warm natural daylight and an upper-body composition. Convey approachability, attentiveness, and professionalism.

## 4 观点文章 安静有思考感 Insights and Articles

### 中文

生成横版 3:2 杂志人物肖像。人物坐在简洁办公桌旁，身体略微侧向画面，目光看向镜头外近处，神情专注自然，避免刻意托腮。桌面只放一本合上的素色笔记本。中景呈现人物与少量环境，手部自然位于画面外。暖米白墙面搭配深木色家具，柔和侧光，整体克制、有层次，避免摆拍式权威感。

### English

Create a horizontal 3:2 editorial portrait suitable for an opinion article. Seat the subject beside a simple desk, with the body angled slightly and the gaze directed just off-camera. The expression is focused and natural; avoid a staged chin-resting pose. Place only a closed, plain notebook on the desk. Use a medium shot showing the subject and a small amount of the surrounding interior, with the hands naturally outside the frame. Warm ivory walls, dark wood furniture, and soft side lighting. Keep the image quiet, restrained, and visually layered, without exaggerated symbols of authority.

## 5 黑白专栏 庄重现代 Black and White Portrait

### 中文

生成竖版 4:5 黑白编辑肖像，胸部以上构图，人物略偏画面一侧，另一侧留少量空间。穿结构简洁的深色商务服装，目光坚定而放松，不刻意皱眉。采用柔和、有方向性的侧光，背景为均匀深灰色。保留丰富灰阶、真实皮肤纹理和清晰眼神，避免电影海报式强烈光影。

### English

Create a vertical 4:5 black-and-white editorial portrait, framed from the chest up. Position the subject slightly off-center, leaving a little breathing room on the opposite side. Use simple, structured dark business attire. The gaze should be assured but relaxed, without a forced frown. Apply soft, directional side lighting against an even dark gray background. Preserve rich tonal gradation, realistic skin texture, and clear, expressive eyes. Avoid dramatic movie-poster lighting.

## 统一排除项 Shared Exclusions

### 中文

不要改变身份特征，不要过度磨皮、塑料皮肤、僵硬笑容或失真比例。不要多余人物、畸形手指、文字、水印、企业或交易所标志、股票代码屏幕、奖杯、证书和奢华权力符号。

### English

Do not alter the subject’s identity or distinctive features. Avoid excessive skin smoothing, plastic-looking skin, stiff smiles, distorted anatomy, extra people, malformed fingers, text, watermarks, corporate or stock-exchange logos, stock ticker screens, trophies, certificates, and ostentatious displays of wealth or power.

## 成组一致性与使用披露 Series Consistency and Disclosure

### 中文

做成一组时，使用同一组清晰原照，保持发型和服装色系一致。生成后逐张核对面部辨识度；这些是创作肖像，不宜配文成真实采访、交易所到访或颁奖现场。

公开使用时，建议明确标注为“AI 创作肖像”，避免让读者误认为真实事件的现场照片。

### English

For a consistent series, use the same clear reference photos and maintain a consistent hairstyle and clothing palette. Check facial likeness in every result. These are AI-created portraits; do not present them as documentary photographs of an actual interview, exchange visit, or award ceremony.

When used publicly, we recommend clearly labeling the image as an “AI-created portrait” so readers do not mistake it for documentary photography of a real event.
